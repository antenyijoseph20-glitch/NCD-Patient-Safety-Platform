How Aid Reaches Patients With Zero Diversion

To guarantee donor and government trust, financial aid is never disbursed as untracked cash. Every grant follows a rigorous three-step verification workflow.

1

Clinical Diagnosis & Evidence

The patient's condition is evaluated by an MDCN-licensed physician at an accredited healthcare facility. Diagnostic proofs (biopsy, histopathology, radiology) are certified with cryptographic checksums.

Physician Authority Remains Sovereign
2

Four-Eyes Grant Approval

Assistance requests are matched against active sponsor guidelines. Under strict separation of duties, no single officer can both apply for and approve aid, guaranteeing zero conflicts of interest.

Institutional Grant Governance
3

Direct-to-Hospital Settlement

Funds are disbursed directly to the treating hospital, diagnostic center, or pharmacy via secure banking protocols (NIBSS NIP) upon receipt of validated treatment and dispensing evidence.

Zero Cash Intermediaries

Sponsored Health Envelopes

Active Healthcare Assistance Funds

Targeted subsidies established by government bodies, philanthropic foundations, and corporate social responsibility (CSR) partners.

Built for High Patronage & Institutional Trust

Why Governments, Donors & Hospitals Partner With Us

Built from the ground up to prevent misuse, certify clinical necessity, and provide live financial transparency.

Zero Cash Diversion

Assistance is never paid to individual bank accounts. All disbursements go directly to verified hospital accounts against verified clinical invoices.

Multi-Tier Governance

Four-Eyes operational controls ensure applications are vetted across clinical leads, case officers, and independent funding authorities.

Real-Time Audit Ledgers

Immutable transactional accounting records every payment, clawback, and fee adjustment with bank gateway tracking numbers.

NDPA Privacy by Design

Patient medical details and identity records are guarded by strict role-based authorization in full compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

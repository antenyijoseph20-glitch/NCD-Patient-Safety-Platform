Unified Dual-Stream Governance

Strict separation of duties ensures clinical decisions belong solely to certified physicians, while financial approvals remain independently audited.

M1

Clinical & Care Delivery Stream

Team A Core

Maintains patient demographic profiles, emergency contacts, caregiver delegation matrix, ICD-10 diagnostic condition staging, and verifiable clinical proofs.

Physician Verification: Only MDCN-licensed professionals at registered facilities can certify clinical needs.
Clinical Evidence Proof: Immutable SHA-256 document checksums for biopsies and radiology scans.
Safety Incident Reporting: Near-miss and critical triage reporting with immediate remediation audits.
M2

Funding, Payments & Governance

Team B Core

Executes donor funding allocations, assistance application reviews, strict Four-Eyes decision approvals, banking payment instructions, and audited bank reconciliation.

Four-Eyes Governance: Applicants cannot approve grants; authorizers cannot execute bank disbursements.
Pre-Funding Eligibility: Enforces verified clinical need and eligible status before financial commitment.
Immutable Financial Ledger: Zero destructive deletions; corrections via PaymentAdjustment records.
